Clicking

Hints

Single, double, and longer hints share the screen. Short hints stay available even with many controls.

Behavior

Type a complete hint to move the pointer and click once. Escape cancels. Controls that move or disappear are never clicked at their old position.

Scrolling

Hold H, J, K, or L to scroll left, down, up, or right. Release to stop. Hold Shift to dash.

Feedback and recovery

Not Allowed

Explain what each permission enables. Offer Grant Access and the exact System Settings pane. Refusal remains stable with no prompt loop.

Needs Attention

Tap failure clears overlays and passes keys through. Restart Input is explicit; no retry occurs in the callback.

Discovery and empty results

“Finding controls… Esc cancels” responds immediately. No supported results or a stale target produces a brief explanation and returns to idle.

Shortcut validation

“Include Command, Option, or Control.” and “Click and Scroll modes need different shortcuts.” preserve the previous valid value. Escape dismisses.

Overlay language

One nonactivating, click-through panel per display. One drawing view per panel. No screen dimming or focus change. Each callout tip marks the exact click point. Badges can sit above, below, left, or right, staying fixed during filtering. Row text and artwork share one hint; embedded controls remain independent. From OverlayController.swift.
